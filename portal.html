How to read this

The base estimate comes from Ames sales recorded from 2006 to 2010. A second figure applies a broad Ames market-index adjustment to approximate 2026 dollars; it is not a newly trained 2026 model or a property-specific forecast. Neighborhood and property prices may have moved differently.

The historical model’s typical absolute error was about $18,000 in its validation sample. The extra uncertainty from applying the market trend is not measured.

The source data covers houses, townhomes, and duplexes—not apartments. The market adjustment uses the FHFA Ames house price index via FRED.
