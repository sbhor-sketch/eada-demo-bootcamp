# Ames Housing data

This folder contains the Ames, Iowa housing data used by Ronald's example price model. The records describe historical home sales and are provided for educational modeling; estimates are not formal appraisals.

- `train.csv`: training records, including sale prices
- `test.csv`: held-out Kaggle competition records
- `sample_submission.csv`: sample output format
- `data_description.txt`: field definitions
